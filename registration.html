Section 8 — FERPA Notice & Records

Your Rights Under FERPA

The Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) affords parents and students over 18 certain rights with respect to the student's education records. These rights include:

  1. The right to inspect and review education records within 45 days of a request.
  2. The right to request amendment of records believed to be inaccurate or misleading.
  3. The right to provide written consent before records are disclosed to third parties, except as authorized by law.
  4. The right to file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Education.

Under Arkansas ADE Rules (351), student permanent records will be maintained until your student receives a high school diploma or is beyond compulsory attendance age. Upon transfer to another school, ZION outSchool will provide a copy of the student's permanent record to the receiving school within 10 school days of request.

Section 11 — Tuition, Fees & Attendance Policy

ZION outSchool operates like a reserved space in a program, not a pay-per-day service. When your child enrolls, their spot is held for them all month. Tuition is $600/month paid in advance. Absences are not deducted from tuition. A 10% late fee applies after a 5-day grace period. Withdrawal requires 30-day written notice. Tuition is non-refundable for the current month.

Tuition & Fees

  • Annual Curriculum Fee: $200 — charged once per year, all students
  • Tuition: $600/month — paid in advance; absences do not reduce tuition
  • Unit Fee: $135/unit × 4 units per year
  • Late Payment: 10% late fee after a 5-day grace period
  • Early Withdrawal Policy:
    • Because ZION outSchool operates through highly individualized instruction, collaborative learning cohorts, and long-term project-based educational design, enrollment represents a year-long commitment between the school and family. Significant staffing, curriculum development, scheduling, and classroom planning decisions are made in reliance upon each student's enrollment for the full academic year.
    • The annual $200 curriculum and materials fee is non-refundable once enrollment is finalized.
    • Tuition paid for the current month is non-refundable upon withdrawal.
    • A $300 withdrawal fee per enrolled student will be assessed at the time of withdrawal to help offset administrative, staffing, and classroom restructuring costs associated with mid-year withdrawal.
    • We understand that unexpected circumstances can arise. Families experiencing significant hardship are encouraged to communicate with the administration, and exceptional situations may be reviewed at the school's discretion.
