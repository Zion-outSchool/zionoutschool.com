Curiosity is contagious.

Younger children often ask the question an older student has learned to keep quiet. They haven’t yet decided that not knowing is embarrassing. Their excitement over a discovery can remind others how much there is to love about learning.

In a mixed-age classroom, that exchange matters. An older student can help a younger classmate make sense of a difficult idea—and then find themselves surprised by what that child notices. Every student brings something the others might have missed.

We choose this setting because we want questions to come freely and students to recognize that they can learn from one another, whatever their age.

Shared learning still makes room for individual needs. In ELA and mathematics, teachers guide students according to their readiness and check understanding as they progress. Belonging to the same classroom does not mean being on the same page.

A fresh unit. A place to begin.

January begins a new unit at Zion, giving incoming students a shared starting point with their classmates.

In ELA and mathematics, students begin at an appropriate level and progress at their own pace with teacher guidance. Your child does not need to be on the same curriculum page as another student to begin learning alongside them.

Before your child starts, we will discuss their current learning and placement, explain expectations, and help your family prepare.

Preparing for Your First Day

Use the guidance below to prepare your child’s laptop, order approved uniforms, and gather personal supplies.

Please review the school’s requirements before making purchases. If you intend to use education funding, confirm that the purchase and payment method meet current program requirements.

Technology: a laptop for each student

Every Zion student needs a dedicated laptop, including early elementary students. Digital tools support selected parts of instruction; many younger students use Beast Academy Online for mathematics.

If your child already has a suitable laptop, you do not need to purchase a new one. Chromebooks are welcome. The device should arrive fully charged each school day.
