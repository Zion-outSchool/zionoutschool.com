What to Expect at Zion

Choosing a school involves more than choosing a curriculum. You need to understand the school day, how teachers work with students, what your family’s responsibilities will be, and how your child will settle in.

Here you can explore daily life at Zion and find practical guidance for preparing to begin.

Daily life at Zion

The weekly schedule

Students learn in person Monday–Thursday, 8 a.m.–3 p.m. Friday is reserved for at-home assignments and reading.

Read about the school day →

The classroom experience

Students learn in a mixed-age classroom, with placement and progress guided by readiness and demonstrated understanding. Reading, writing, student presentations, and project work are part of learning at Zion.

See How We Learn →

Faith in daily learning

Scripture anchors learning at Zion. The school’s foundation is in the teachings of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

Read Our Foundation →

School and home working together

Families support daily reading, oversee Friday work, communicate about attendance and student needs, and stay engaged with their child’s learning.

Read about parent responsibilities →

Joining Midyear

Our enrollment conversation begins with your child: their current learning, strengths, challenges, and what your family hopes will be different.

Before enrollment is finalized, we will discuss placement, expectations, tuition, and payment arrangements. Once your child’s place is confirmed, we will provide the curriculum and preparation information needed for their start.

Preparing for Your First Day

Use the guidance below to prepare your child’s laptop, order approved uniforms, and gather personal supplies.

Please review the school’s requirements before making purchases. If you intend to use education funding, confirm that the purchase and payment method meet current program requirements.

Technology: a reliable laptop

Students use a laptop daily for research, writing, Google Classroom, and project work. Any functional laptop is acceptable — Chromebooks included. It should arrive fully charged each day.

Notice of Nondiscriminatory Policy as to Students

ZION outSchool admits students of any race, color, national and ethnic origin to all the rights, privileges, programs, and activities generally accorded or made available to students at the school. ZION outSchool does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national and ethnic origin in the administration of its educational policies, admissions policies, scholarship and financial-assistance programs, and athletic and other school-administered programs.
