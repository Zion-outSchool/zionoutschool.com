What It Costs
Monthly Tuition $600

Paid in advance. No deductions for absences.

Unit Fee $135

Per unit x 4 units per year. Covers all books, materials, printing, subscriptions, and portfolio supplies.

Annual Curriculum Fee $200

Charged once per year. Includes mathematics curriculum selection and placement testing.

New students receive an initial teacher assessment at no additional cost. If a student advances into a new mathematics curriculum level during the year, an additional curriculum fee will apply to cover new materials.

Annual Total

$6,740

Tuition + Unit Fees + Annual Curriculum Fee

Fully covered by EFA for qualifying families

$468 remaining for uniform and supplies after all fees
