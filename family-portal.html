Notice of Nondiscriminatory Policy as to Students

ZION outSchool admits students of any race, color, national and ethnic origin to all the rights, privileges, programs, and activities generally accorded or made available to students at the school. ZION outSchool does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national and ethnic origin in the administration of its educational policies, admissions policies, scholarship and financial-assistance programs, and athletic and other school-administered programs.
