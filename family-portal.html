Placement Testing

Every child begins where they are ready.

At ZION outSchool, curriculum placement is based on a student's demonstrated readiness rather than age or grade level alone. A student may be working at different levels in different subjects.

Our goal is not to produce the highest possible placement score. It is to identify the place where the student can learn successfully, build confidence, and progress toward mastery.

Before You Begin

  • Review all directions.
  • Gather permitted materials.
  • Choose a quiet time and location.
  • Contact ZION before testing if your child needs accommodations.
  • Do not preview or teach the test questions beforehand.

While Your Child Is Testing

Please encourage your child to do their best, but allow them to complete the work independently. Do not explain concepts, suggest strategies, point out errors, reword academic questions, or ask the child to reconsider an answer.

If assistance is required to understand a general direction, note exactly what assistance was given when submitting the test.

Remember

This assessment is not something your child must pass. Helping may unintentionally place your child in curriculum that is too difficult and make learning more frustrating. An honest picture of current skills allows us to choose the right starting point.

Submit the Assessment

The upload form should collect:

  • Parent name
  • Student name
  • Assessment subject and title
  • Date completed
  • Approximate completion time
  • Breaks taken
  • Accommodations or assistance provided
  • Parent observations
  • File upload
  • Parent certification

Parent Certification

I confirm that my child completed this assessment independently except for the assistance or accommodations described above. The submitted pages represent my child's original work and have not been corrected or revised.

What happens afterward

What Happens Next?

  • ZION reviews the completed assessment and written work.
  • Staff may request a follow-up assessment, reading sample, or conversation.
  • Placement is determined using the full body of evidence, not necessarily the test company's score alone.
  • The family receives the recommended curriculum level.
  • Curriculum is ordered only after ZION confirms placement.
  • Placement may be adjusted after the student begins if classroom performance shows that a different level is more appropriate.

A placement assessment is an informed starting point, not an irreversible label. Please do not purchase curriculum until placement has been confirmed.
