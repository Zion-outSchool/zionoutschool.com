Tuition & Funding

A serious education should be financially possible.

Understanding the cost of school should be straightforward. Below you’ll find Zion’s tuition, what each fee provides, and funding options your family may be able to use.

Zion outSchool participates in Arkansas’s Education Freedom Account program. Funding may cover some or all of a student’s tuition and eligible expenses, depending on approval and available funds.

Considering a January start? We would be glad to discuss your child, explain the costs, and help you understand your next steps.

2026–27 tuition and fees

Monthly tuition

$600

Tuition supports your child’s instruction and daily learning at Zion. It is paid in advance, and absences do not reduce tuition.

2026–27 Curriculum Fee — $200 per student

This fee supports the initial core curriculum your child will use during the 2026–27 school year. It applies to students joining in January and is separate from tuition.

If your family chooses to return for 2027–28, a separate Enrollment & Curriculum Fee will apply to that school year. Next year’s amount and terms will be announced before re-enrollment opens.

The Curriculum Fee is not credited toward tuition. It becomes nonrefundable when Zion confirms your child’s place. If Zion cannot provide the confirmed place, the fee will be refunded.

Unit fee

$135 per unit

Unit fees provide Outstanding Works of Literature (OWLs) selected for each unit, along with unit-specific materials and subscriptions. Students are charged only for units in which they participate.

What the curriculum and unit fees provide

Core curriculum provided through the Curriculum Fee

  • One initial English language arts curriculum
  • One initial mathematics curriculum
  • Science and history curriculum resources for the full school year

Unit fees provide

  • OWLs selected for that unit
  • Materials specific to that unit
  • Subscriptions specific to that unit

Students progress through ELA and mathematics according to their readiness. If your child completes the initially provided curriculum, your family is responsible for purchasing the next curriculum. Teachers will discuss the next step and its cost with you before a purchase is needed.

Starting in January?

You do not need to wait until a new school year to explore Zion.

For students joining in January, charges include tuition beginning with enrollment, the 2026–27 Curriculum Fee, and fees for the units in which the student participates.

Before your family finalizes enrollment, we will confirm your child’s placement, provide an itemized explanation of charges, and discuss payment arrangements and any available education funding.

Using Arkansas EFA funding

Zion is an EFA-participating school. Your family’s available funding depends on program approval, funds awarded, and any funds already used.

Already approved for an EFA?

If your child currently receives EFA funding, contact us about a January transfer. We will help you identify the steps to confirm your available balance and arrange payment to Zion.

Seeking funding for the current school year?

We are confirming whether new applications and funding are available for students entering in January 2027. Please contact us to discuss your circumstances. We cannot guarantee a midyear award.

Planning for 2027–28?

The anticipated EFA application window is March 1–June 1, 2027. These dates are estimates and have not yet been officially confirmed. Application and renewal information will be updated when available.

School enrollment and EFA approval are separate processes. Confirm your funding and payment arrangements with Zion before finalizing enrollment.

Zion scholarship assistance

Families with a remaining tuition gap may apply for Zion’s need-based scholarship assistance.

Awards depend on demonstrated financial need and available scholarship resources. Applying does not guarantee an award or full coverage of a funding gap.

Looking ahead to 2027–28

Returning families will be invited to re-enroll before the current school year ends.

An Annual Enrollment & Curriculum Fee will reserve your child’s place for 2027–28 and support advance planning and core curriculum purchases. It is separate from tuition and is not credited toward tuition.

The fee amount, payment deadline, coverage, and refund terms will be provided before re-enrollment opens. Any transition arrangements for current families will be communicated directly.
