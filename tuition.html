For most Arkansas families, EFA funding can cover the full cost of tuition and required school fees. Additional scholarship pathways may be available for qualifying students.

Arkansas Education Freedom Account

For qualifying Arkansas families, tuition and all required school expenses are fully covered by the EFA — with funds remaining for uniform and supplies.

Every K–12 student in Arkansas is eligible. The EFA provides $7,208 per student for the 2026–2027 school year, deposited quarterly into a ClassWallet account. ZION outSchool is a confirmed EFA-participating school. Your family's out-of-pocket cost can be zero.

How the EFA Works

Arkansas's Education Freedom Account deposits $1,802 per quarter into your ClassWallet account for the 2026–2027 school year. You direct those funds to ZION outSchool for tuition, unit fees, and the annual curriculum fee. Remaining funds — approximately $468 — can be applied toward your student's required uniform and other approved educational expenses.

Applications for the 2026–2027 school year are open through June 1.

Apply at the Arkansas Department of Education →

Already have an EFA? You must renew every year. Funding is not automatically continued. Returning families complete their renewal survey in ClassWallet before June 1. Missing this deadline means losing funding for the entire school year.

The Annual Curriculum Fee is invoiced at the start of each school year. Unit fees are invoiced at the start of each unit. Student supplies list provided prior to the start of term.

Additional Scholarship Pathway

Philanthropic Investment in Arkansas Kids (PIAK)

Some Arkansas families may qualify for additional scholarship support beyond the EFA through the PIAK scholarship. PIAK is income-based and administered through The Reform Alliance. In most cases, families choose either EFA or PIAK. However, some income-qualifying families with a documented student disability may be able to combine both funding sources.

ZION outSchool is actively working toward becoming a PIAK-participating school. If your family may qualify, we encourage you to begin exploring the process early.
