Who Teaches Here

Adults who still know
how to learn.

The teachers at ZION outSchool are not lecturers delivering information from a distance. They are mentors, investigators, and disciplined thinkers who help students pursue truth with humility, courage, and intellectual rigor.

Who We Hire

Teaching at ZION outSchool is not a job for someone looking for a classroom to manage. It is a calling for someone who has never stopped being a learner.

Every instructor is selected against one standard: do they pursue truth the way we ask our students to? Not just in their subject area — across disciplines, across texts, across the boundary between academic knowledge and lived faith.

What that looks like in practice:

  • A baccalaureate degree or demonstrated equivalent expertise in their field
  • Deep familiarity with the LDS standard works and the capacity to teach from scripture as a primary academic text — not as a devotional supplement
  • Fluency in interdisciplinary thinking — the ability to move between science, history, literature, and theology without losing the thread
  • A genuine belief that children are capable of serious intellectual work, and the patience and skill to hold them to it
  • The humility to ask questions they do not yet know the answers to — in front of students

Our Faculty

Sandra J. Isenberger

Founder, Director & Lead Facilitator

Sandra built this school because she believes a child's education should strengthen their faith — not compete with it.

Sandra J. Isenberger founded ZION outSchool from thirty years of teaching in homeschool, seminary, and church education settings — environments where the connection between faith and learning is not an add-on but the foundation.

She has only taught briefly in a conventional classroom. That is not a gap in her experience. It is the source of her freedom to build something genuinely different.

Her academic formation is deliberately interdisciplinary: a Bachelor of Science in Elementary Education and a Master of Science in Biological Secondary Education. She is currently pursuing coursework in neurological education through Johns Hopkins University, with plans to continue into a graduate program in that field. She understands the structure of living systems, the architecture of learning environments, and the emerging science of how the brain actually receives and retains truth. These are not separate credentials. They are the same preparation, arrived at from different angles.

The curriculum she has built reflects all of it — science that begins in scripture, inquiry that trusts both reason and revelation, and a classroom designed around how children are actually made to learn.

Sandra built this school because she believes a child's education should strengthen their faith — not compete with it.

Betty Jo Smith

Teacher & Facilitator

Betty Jo loves educating students, helping them discover their passions, and watching them recognize God's hand in their education.

Betty Jo Smith has spent the past fourteen years teaching middle and high school in public education. She began teaching as a way to remain involved in her own children's education and help them become successful.

She earned a Bachelor of Science in Career and Technical Education, with an emphasis in Family and Consumer Sciences, from the University of Arkansas.

One of Betty Jo's favorite quotes comes from Nelson Mandela: "Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world." She believes true education inspires students to recognize the greatness of God's works while equipping them to understand, appreciate, and thoughtfully contribute to the world.

Betty Jo is looking forward to teaching at ZION outSchool, where students grow in wisdom as they study the works of God and gain a deeper understanding of the world He created. She loves educating students and helping them discover their passions. She believes in the students she teaches and looks forward to watching them reach their educational goals while recognizing God's hand in their education.

Betty Jo is married and has three daughters and two grandchildren. She enjoys gardening, spending time with her family, good food, traveling, playing the piano, and singing with her daughters.

Karen Phillips

Teacher & Facilitator

Karen brings more than twenty years of experience in early childhood education, special education, and hands-on youth programming.

Karen comes to ZION outSchool from the University of Arkansas Extension Office, bringing more than twenty years of experience in early childhood education, special education, and hands-on youth programming.

She began her career as a home-based teacher with Head Start, working closely with families to prepare young children academically, socially, and emotionally for school. She later spent nearly ten years supporting elementary students in special education, including students with dyslexia and children who benefited from individualized, multisensory instruction.

At the University of Arkansas Extension Office, Karen delivered garden, agriculture, nutrition, and STEM education to hundreds of elementary students. She taught subjects such as embryology, entomology, plant science, food preparation, and public speaking, while using school gardens as living laboratories where children could learn through direct experience.

Karen chose education because she wanted to make a direct and lasting impact on the minds and character of the next generation. She believes academic learning and spiritual formation should be seamlessly woven together, with scriptural truth serving not as an isolated subject but as the foundation of a child's education.

She is drawn to ZION outSchool because students are invited to recognize the connected nature of truth — to see God's hand in scripture, science, history, literature, and the physical world. Karen wants students not merely to memorize information, but to think critically, ask meaningful questions, and discover their God-given potential.

Tracy Keeney

Music Director

Tracy is a music educator, composer, and choir director with nearly forty years of experience inspiring students of all ages.

Tracy Keeney is a music educator, composer, and choir director with nearly forty years of experience inspiring students of all ages.

She has directed children's, youth, and adult choirs for more than three decades. Her original compositions have been published and performed by choirs across the nation, and she spent ten years presenting workshops throughout Kansas and Missouri on music's effects on the body, mind, and spirit.

Tracy believes music is far more than an enrichment subject. It strengthens memory and focus, develops confidence and creativity, teaches children to listen and work together, and gives them a meaningful way to express what they feel.

At ZION outSchool, she will help students build strong musical foundations through singing, rhythm, notation, movement, instruments, harmony, and performance. Students will encounter music from many styles and cultures — including classical masterworks, sacred and patriotic music, folk traditions, jazz, musical theatre, and carefully selected contemporary music.

Tracy is committed to helping every child participate, grow in confidence, and discover their unique musical abilities. Whether a student becomes a musician or simply develops a lifelong love of music, her hope is that each child will recognize music as one of God's great gifts — a gift that teaches, uplifts, and unites.
