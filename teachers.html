OUR FOUNDATION

Education rooted in revealed truth.

We believe faith and learning are not separate pursuits. Every subject becomes part of the search for truth, wisdom, and discipleship.

Who Teaches Here

Teaching at ZION outSchool is not a job for someone looking for a classroom to manage. It is a calling for someone who has never stopped being a learner.

Every instructor at ZION outSchool is selected against one standard: do they pursue truth the way we ask our students to? Not just in their subject area — across disciplines, across texts, across the boundary between academic knowledge and lived faith.

What that looks like in practice:

  • A baccalaureate degree or demonstrated equivalent expertise in their field
  • Deep familiarity with the LDS standard works and the capacity to teach from scripture as a primary academic text — not as a devotional supplement
  • Fluency in interdisciplinary thinking — the ability to move between science, history, literature, and theology without losing the thread
  • A genuine belief that children are capable of serious intellectual work, and the patience and skill to hold them to it
  • The humility to ask questions they do not yet know the answers to — in front of students

Sandra J. Isenberger

Founder, Director & Lead Facilitator

Sandra built this school because she believes a child's education should strengthen their faith, not compete with it.

Sandra J. Isenberger founded ZION outSchool from thirty years of teaching in homeschool, seminary, and church education settings — environments where the connection between faith and learning is not an add-on but the foundation.


She has only taught briefly in a conventional classroom. That is not a gap in her experience. It is the source of her freedom to build something genuinely different.


Her academic formation is deliberately interdisciplinary: a Bachelor of Science in Elementary Education and a Master of Science in Biological Secondary Education. She is currently pursuing coursework in neurological education through Johns Hopkins University, with plans to continue into a graduate program in that field. She understands the structure of living systems, the architecture of learning environments, and the emerging science of how the brain actually receives and retains truth. These are not separate credentials. They are the same preparation, arrived at from different angles.


The curriculum she has built reflects all of it — science that begins in scripture, inquiry that trusts both reason and revelation, and a classroom designed around how children are actually made to learn.
