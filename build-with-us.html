Who We Are

A real school. A real classroom.
Real results, already.

ZION outSchool is a Christ-centered private school in Fayetteville, Arkansas, currently serving students in Kindergarten through 12th grade. We are in our founding year — and the classroom is already producing the kind of learning we promised.

A nine year old independently arrived at controlled experimental design — two canal systems, one variable changed, one held constant, three trials, averaged results, written conclusion — without being taught the methodology. He found it because genuine inquiry produces it naturally when the conditions are right.

A student studied the Nile River — its chemistry, its hydrology, its agricultural history — and connected it to the live water dispute between Ethiopia, Egypt, and Sudan threatening military conflict today. Then helped fund a real borehole well in Sudan. The learning left the room.

A seventh grader designed an experiment to feel thermal equilibration before she could name it scientifically. She named it later. The understanding came first — because she found it herself.

These are not aspirations. They happened this year, in this classroom, with real children whose parents trusted us with the most important work of their lives.

Who Built This

Thirty years in the making.

Sandra J. Isenberger founded ZION outSchool after thirty years of teaching in homeschool, seminary, and church education settings — environments where the connection between faith and learning is not an add-on but the foundation.

Her academic formation is deliberately interdisciplinary: a lifetime spent studying scripture, a Bachelor of Science in Elementary Education, a Master of Science in Biological Secondary Education, and ongoing study of neurological education through Johns Hopkins University. She understands the structure of living systems, the architecture of learning environments, and the emerging science of how children actually receive and retain truth.

She did not build this school because the existing options were almost right. She built it because she believed children deserve something genuinely different — and because thirty years of teaching had shown her exactly what that looked like.

What We Are Building Together

Students who leave the classroom
changed — and obligated.

At ZION outSchool, students do not simply learn about problems. Every unit culminates in outward-facing work — something real, made from what was genuinely understood, sent into the world for people who were not in the room when the learning happened.

Researching global water scarcity at a molecular, historical, and theological level — then helping fund a real borehole well in Sudan.

Presenting original research publicly during colloquium, without notes, before peers who ask hard questions and expect honest revision.

Working alongside experts in science, architecture, literature, and theology — discovering what disciplined work and faithful living actually look like from the inside.

The goal is not simply educated students. The goal is capable young people with the knowledge, character, and experience to meaningfully bless the world. And that requires something the school alone cannot provide.

Why Partnership Matters

What only you can give them.

We can teach a student to think historically, write analytically, reason scientifically, and defend a position under pressure. What we cannot give them is what you carry — the specific knowledge of what it actually feels like to do the work you do, in the field you chose, over the years it took to become who you are.

A student who has only read about engineering has not met an engineer who loves it. A student who has only studied medicine has not sat with a physician who chose it. That gap — between knowledge and encounter — is exactly where you come in. You are not supplementing what happens in this classroom. You are completing it.

By graduation, we want every student to carry:

✦

Real experience — not hypothetical preparation

✦

Real mentorship — relationships with people doing meaningful work

✦

Real capability — not credentials, but demonstrated competence

✦

Real direction — a path already underway, not a blank page at graduation

None of those four things can come entirely from a classroom. Every single one of them requires a person — someone like you — who has already walked a path worth following.
