What We Are Building Together

Students who leave the classroom
changed — and obligated.

At ZION outSchool, students do not simply learn about problems. Every unit culminates in outward-facing work — something real, made from what was genuinely understood, sent into the world for people who were not in the room when the learning happened.

Researching global water scarcity at a molecular, historical, and theological level — then helping fund a real borehole well in Sudan.

Presenting original research publicly during colloquium, without notes, before peers who ask hard questions and expect honest revision.

Working alongside experts in science, architecture, literature, and theology — discovering what disciplined work and faithful living actually look like from the inside.

The goal is not simply educated students. The goal is capable young people with the knowledge, character, and experience to meaningfully bless the world.

Why Partnership Matters

Students need more than classrooms.

A student exploring engineering should meet engineers. A student studying literature should encounter real writers. A student drawn to medicine, architecture, agriculture, or the trades should spend time with people doing meaningful work in those fields.

The more students encounter mastery, craftsmanship, expertise, and real responsibility during their formative years, the more clearly they begin to understand who they are, what they love, and how they can contribute.

By graduation, we want every student to carry:

✦

Real experience — not hypothetical preparation

✦

Real mentorship — relationships with people doing meaningful work

✦

Real capability — not credentials, but demonstrated competence

✦

Real direction — a path already underway, not a blank page at graduation

That takes a community. That is what we are building.

"Everything your child learns here is intended to leave this room."
— Sandra J. Isenberger, Founder

Ways to Get Involved

There are many ways
to build with us.

01

Mentor a Student

Guide a student exploring a field, vocation, or area of inquiry through conversation, encouragement, and real-world insight. This is one of the most significant things you can offer a young person who is genuinely trying to find their path.

02

Offer Apprenticeships or Internships

Provide meaningful hands-on experience for high school students as they begin discovering the kind of work they may be called to do. Real work in a real environment with a real professional changes what a student believes is possible for them.

03

Share Your Expertise

Speak to a class, lead a workshop, participate in colloquium, or simply answer questions from students engaged in genuine inquiry. You do not need to be available every week. One conversation at the right moment can change the direction of a student's thinking.

04

Partner as a College or Institution

Collaborate through dual enrollment, concurrent credit pathways, faculty mentorship, guest lectures, student research opportunities, workshops, or specialized coursework. We are building pathways that give students a genuine head start.

05

Sponsor a Unit or Student Project

Help fund materials, guest experts, field experiences, or the outward-facing student projects that connect learning to real community needs. Every unit ends with something that leaves the classroom — you can be part of what it becomes.

06

Support Scholarships

Help make rigorous, faith-centered education accessible to families who could not otherwise participate. Every scholarship that is funded is a child whose trajectory changes. That is not a small thing.

07

Give Financially

Support facilities, teachers, technology, curriculum, operations, and the long-term stability of ZION outSchool. The school you are reading about exists because people have chosen to fund it. Your contribution sustains and expands what is possible here.

08

Donate Goods or Services

Contribute books, tools, furniture, equipment, printing, technology, building materials, workspace, transportation, or professional services. We are building carefully with limited resources. In-kind contributions make a direct and immediate difference.

The Honest Picture

We are building carefully.
And courageously.

Like many mission-driven schools in their formative years, ZION outSchool is building something ambitious with limited resources. We are working to secure facilities, support teachers sustainably, expand student opportunities, provide scholarships, and strengthen the partnerships that make the vision real.

Every contribution — financial, relational, intellectual, or practical — helps create opportunities for students that would not otherwise exist. You are not funding overhead. You are funding the formation of a generation of people who will seek truth, build well, and bless the world.
