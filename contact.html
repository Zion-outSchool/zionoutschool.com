START HERE

Let’s begin with your child.

Choosing a school is not just a paperwork decision. It is a question of fit, trust, formation, and belonging. Tell us about your student, and we will help you discern whether ZION outSchool is the right place to grow.

Your Child Has a Place Here

We currently serve students in grades 2 through 8. Kindergarten, 1st grade, and high school programs are forming as interest grows.

We are enrolling now for the coming school year. Capacity is intentionally limited so students can be known, challenged, and mentored well.

Begin by telling us about your student. We will listen carefully, answer honestly, and help you understand whether this community is the right fit.

1. Tell Us About Your Student

Share your child’s age, grade, learning needs, strengths, and what you are hoping to find in a school.

2. Begin a Conversation

We will follow up personally to answer questions, discuss fit, and help you understand the next step.

3. Complete Enrollment

When your family is ready to move forward, we will guide you through registration, funding, and required documents.

What You’ll Need Later

You do not need to gather every document before reaching out. When your family is ready to complete enrollment, these records help us finalize your student’s file:

  • Immunization record
  • Individualized Education Program / IEP, if applicable
  • Court custody records, if applicable

ZION outSchool will be located in the Fayetteville, Arkansas area for the 2026–2027 school year. Our space is being finalized. ZION outSchool is an EFA-participating private learning institution; formal private school registration and accreditation are in process.
