An education that deepens understanding and strengthens faith.

At ZION outSchool, students build strong academic foundations while learning to pursue questions of their own. Small classes give teachers time to understand each student’s progress and adapt instruction when a different approach is needed.

Scripture anchors our school day. Through thoughtful study, students discover how faith informs their understanding of the world.

In-person learning Monday–Thursday, 8 a.m.–3 p.m. Friday learning continues at home.

Strong foundations open the door to deeper questions.

In middle and high school science, students study Cambridge course material and connect it to current scientific research through extended investigations called QUESTs.

Could a kidney be preserved by freezing—and still function afterward?

Students study current research from the scientific community, using their Cambridge coursework and laboratory observations to understand the science behind it. They examine what researchers have demonstrated and what remains uncertain, then explain their conclusions using evidence.

Teachers check understanding along the way. When students need further instruction, we revisit the concept and provide another opportunity to demonstrate learning.
