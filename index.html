Truth. Discovered. Lived. Shared.

ZION outSchool is a Christ-centered private school where Scripture is the foundation, inquiry is the method, and every subject becomes part of the search for eternal truth and the application of wisdom.

This Week in the Classroom

These are not hypotheticals.

A second grader builds a physical model of the human blood system to prove that water cannot do what blood does.

A third grader constructs clay riverbeds to test whether the shape of the Nile determined what the river carried — and where.

A seventh grader designs an experiment to feel thermal equilibration before she can name it scientifically.

Students here do not fill out worksheets and move on. They investigate, present, debate, write analytically, revise their thinking, and create work that matters beyond the classroom.

"We are not building students who have learned things. We are building students who have become someone."
— Sandra J. Isenberger, Founder

Tuition & Funding

For most Arkansas families,
EFA funding covers the full cost.

ZION outSchool is an EFA-participating private learning institution. The Arkansas Education Freedom Account can cover tuition and required school fees — with funds remaining for required supplies.
